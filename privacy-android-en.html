Privacy policy — Nový směr for Android

Operator

Juraj Pavol. Contact: jurajpavol@infini-systems.cz. This policy covers Android application cz.novysmer.app. Date: 15 October 2026.

No account or tracking

No account, advertising, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK or developer-operated server is used.

Private records

Journeys, reasons, reflections and settings are stored locally with AES-GCM encryption. Android Keystore protects the key. App locking uses device security. System cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for app data.

Health Connect

Only with voluntary permission, we read steps, sleep including available stages, and exercise. Values are displayed in app memory, not sent to the developer or third parties, written back or included in backups. We do not use them for advertising, sale, profiling or model training. Revoke permissions in Health Connect or choose Disconnect.

Weather and city search

Weather is optional. Manually entered city names are handled by the Android geocoder, whose provider depends on the device. After city selection, MET Norway receives city coordinates rounded to three decimals and your IP address. We do not use GPS or attach journal or Health Connect data. MET Norway states that IP addresses and coordinates are retained in operational logs in Norway. Forecast caching reduces requests. Your selected city is retained in encrypted app state until you clear it.

Widget

The default widget hides statistics. Showing the day count is optional, and the journey name requires separate consent. Others may see these on your home screen. App lock, an unconfirmed import, or seven days without opening the app hides statistics. Refresh timing also depends on the operating system and launcher.

Manual backups

Exports contain app records and settings but no Health Connect data. They are password-encrypted; the password cannot be recovered. You choose the destination through the system file picker. A chosen cloud provider has its own terms. An encrypted local copy of the previous state is retained until an import is confirmed.

Deletion

Settings allows deletion of one journey or all local data. Deleting all data removes the encrypted file, import rollback copy, encryption key and weather cache. Delete exported files separately. Original Health Connect records are not deleted by this app.

Support and external links

If you email support, we receive your message and contact details through email providers. The app does not send messages itself or attach your journal. Research links open in an external browser and are subject to each website’s terms.

Age and purpose

For adults aged 18+. Content is general education and wellbeing information, not diagnosis, treatment or a personal health prediction. Contact support with privacy questions or requests.
