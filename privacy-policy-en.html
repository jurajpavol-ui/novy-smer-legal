2. Purpose of the app

New Direction is a wellbeing and lifestyle app for adults. It lets you keep one or more journeys with your own start date, record check-ins, slips (returns), urge events with optional intensity (e.g. 0–10) and triggers, optionally enter costs and savings, create pledges / if-then intentions, and optionally store an SOS contact (name and/or phone) for emergencies. It also lets you read general information summarized from publicly available research and public-health sources cited directly in the app.

The app is not a medical device or medical service. It does not provide diagnosis, measurement of health, treatment, dosing, tapering, or a detox plan. It does not replace professional help.

3. Data processed by the app

No personal data is sent to our servers. The app has no account, cloud synchronization, advertising, or first-party analytics. Data stays on your device except when you initiate an external action (opening an external link, dialing a number, or exporting your own backup).

The following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • one or more journeys, including start date and optional description,
  • check-ins and short personal notes,
  • slips (records of a return),
  • urge events with optional intensity (e.g. 0–10) and triggers,
  • optional costs and savings (amounts),
  • optional pledges / if-then intentions,
  • an optional SOS contact — name and/or phone,
  • your visual style selection (from 8 available styles),
  • versioned acknowledgement timestamps (18+, methodology, and any alcohol warning),
  • the optional app-lock setting (Face ID or device passcode).

This data remains in an encrypted app file (AES-GCM, with its key in the iOS Keychain), protected by iOS file protection and excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups.

You can manually export a JSON backup through the iOS share sheet. That file leaves the app sandbox and is written to a location you choose — it is no longer protected by the app-storage encryption, and you are responsible for storing it safely. On import, your journeys, check-ins, slips, urges, costs and pledges are restored, but versioned acknowledgements (18+, methodology, alcohol) are not transferred and the current notices will be shown again after import.

4. Data we do not collect

  • we do not operate user accounts,
  • we do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or advertising identifiers,
  • we do not use HealthKit, cross-app tracking, or advertising SDKs,
  • we do not upload your personal records.

5. Links outside the app

The app may ask iOS to open public HTTPS sources (research and public-health pages) in the system browser or open the Phone app for Czech emergency numbers (155 and 112), the Czech National Quitline, or an optional SOS contact. These actions are always initiated by you. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Biometrics

If you enable the app lock, authentication uses the iOS system interface (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). We do not receive or store biometric data; authentication is handled entirely by Apple systems.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete the app or choose “Delete all data” inside the app. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app removes the local records.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to people under 18, including because its general educational section discusses substance-related topics.
