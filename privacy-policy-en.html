2. Purpose of the app

New Direction is a wellbeing app for adults. It shows elapsed time from a chosen start, estimated savings and general information about changing habits, with source links. Optional features display Apple Health data and weather for a manually selected city.

Research periods are not personal measurements or recovery predictions. The app does not provide diagnosis, treatment, dosing or detox instructions. It does not use Health data to confirm that a research finding has occurred in an individual.

3. Local data and backups

The app has no developer-operated server for Health values or stored journeys. No account is required. The app has no advertising, cross-app tracking or first-party analytics.

Your device stores journeys, start dates, optional personal reasons and daily costs, age and educational acknowledgements, and app-lock settings. Personal notes, past craving records, contacts and other legacy values may remain from earlier versions or imported backups even when the current interface no longer offers entry for them. This update removes slip history, including during import, while preserving the existing effective counter start and savings.

Journey storage uses AES-GCM encryption with its key in the iOS Keychain. That file has iOS file protection and is excluded from system backups. The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

A manual export is a readable, unencrypted JSON file. It includes journeys, any retained legacy records and settings stored with them. Health records, the Health overview preference and the selected city are not included in this backup. You choose its destination or recipient in the system share sheet, including your own cloud storage if desired. We do not manage or delete copies outside the app. Import requires the current age, methodology and any alcohol acknowledgements again.

The Health overview preference and selected city, including its coordinates, are stored separately in local app settings. These are not copies of Health records or the device's GPS location.

4. Optional Apple Health connection

With your permission, the app can use Apple HealthKit to read available sleep records and stages, steps, walking and running distance, and workout information including duration, distance (including cycling and swimming), active energy and available heart rate. You control access through Apple system settings. The main counter and knowledge library do not require this connection.

Health values are processed only in memory to display overviews and summaries. The app does not write them back to Health, store them with journeys, include them in exports or send them to the developer. It does not use them for advertising or tracking.

Disconnecting the Health overview clears its loaded values. This does not change the original records in Apple Health; you can revoke permission separately in Apple settings. Any iCloud synchronization or backup of Apple Health itself is managed by Apple according to your settings, independently of New Direction.

5. Weather and internet services

City search uses Apple MapKit. Weather requests send the selected city's coordinates to Apple WeatherKit to retrieve current conditions and forecasts. The app does not request access to the device's GPS location. After a city is selected, weather may refresh while you use the app.

These features require internet access, and Apple services process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. These requests do not send your Health values, personal journeys or city-search history to the developer. You can remove the selected city in the weather detail.

You initiate external source/support links and calls by tapping them. The destination website or telephone service has its own policies. The counter and bundled knowledge work offline.

6. App lock

The optional lock uses iOS authentication (Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode). We do not receive or store biometric data.

7. Deletion

You can delete local app data in Settings. Successful deletion removes stored journeys, disconnects the Health overview and clears the selected city. It does not alter Apple Health records or previously exported files, which you manage separately. Uninstalling removes the app's local storage.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 or over and is not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy
