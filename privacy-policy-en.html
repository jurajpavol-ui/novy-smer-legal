2. Purpose of the app

New Direction is a wellbeing and lifestyle app for adults. It lets you manually track time from a date you choose, keep short private notes, and read general information summarized from publicly available research and public-health sources.

The app is not a medical device or medical service. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, detox plans, dosing, or personal health predictions.

3. Data processed by the app

No personal data is sent to our servers. The app has no account, cloud synchronization, advertising, or first-party analytics.

The following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • the selected counter and start date,
  • optional personal notes and intentions,
  • your visual style selection,
  • the optional app-lock setting (Face ID or device passcode).

This data remains in an encrypted app file (AES-GCM, with its key in the iOS Keychain), protected by iOS file protection and excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups.

4. Data we do not collect

  • we do not operate user accounts,
  • we do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or advertising identifiers,
  • we do not use HealthKit, cross-app tracking, or advertising SDKs,
  • we do not upload your personal records.

5. Links outside the app

The app may ask iOS to open public HTTPS sources in the system browser or open the Phone app for Czech emergency numbers (155 and 112). Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Biometrics

If you enable the app lock, authentication uses the iOS system interface (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). We do not receive or store biometric data; authentication is handled entirely by Apple systems.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete the app or choose “Delete all data” inside the app. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app removes the local records.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 17, including because its general educational section discusses substance-related topics.
