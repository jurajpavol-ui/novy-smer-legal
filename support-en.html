Suggest a feature

You can optionally suggest a feature by email to jurajpavol@infini-systems.cz. The button opens your email app; you write and review the message and decide whether to send it. The app does not automatically attach any files, Health values or journey data. If you send the email, the publisher receives the content you wrote and the sender contact details provided by your email for replying and considering the suggestion. This voluntary communication requires no app account and is not automatic collection of app data.

What the app offers

  • An elapsed-time counter, multiple journeys and savings estimated from your own entries.
  • Information about alcohol, cigarettes, nicotine vaping, cannabis and cocaine, organized by topic and research period, with further reading and sources.
  • An optional Apple Health connection: sleep and sleep stages, steps, distance and workouts; daily details, weekly statistics and monthly summaries grouped by week.
  • Current weather and forecasts for upcoming days for a manually selected city.
  • A consistent green appearance and three tabs: Overview, Knowledge and Settings.
  • An optional iOS authentication lock and manual backup import/export.

Privacy

The app does not send loaded Health values or stored journey data to developer servers. The app requires no account and has no developer-operated server, cloud synchronization, advertising or first-party analytics.

Journeys are encrypted on your device. Values read from Apple Health are displayed from memory only and are excluded from exports. Weather and city search use Apple internet services; the app does not request device GPS access. See the privacy policy for details, including legacy records.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to connect Health or own an Apple Watch?
No. The HealthKit connection is entirely optional. The counter, savings and knowledge work without it. Available Health values depend on what is recorded in Apple Health and which read permissions you grant.

Why is a Health value missing?
There may be no record for the selected period, or read access may not be granted. A missing record is not treated as zero. Check the original records and Apple system permissions, then refresh the overview.

Does disconnecting or deleting the app erase my Apple Health data?
No. The app does not write to Health. Disconnecting clears only its loaded overview; you manage original records and permissions through Apple.

How is my data backed up?
The app does not provide its own cloud backup. A manual backup includes journeys, any retained legacy records and settings stored with them as a readable JSON file at a destination you choose. It excludes Health data, the Health overview preference and the selected city. Any iCloud synchronization of Apple Health is managed independently by Apple according to your settings.

How do I delete my data?
Choose deletion of all data in Settings. This removes local journeys, disconnects the Health overview and clears the selected city. It does not delete previously exported files or original Apple Health records.

Does the app work offline?
The counter, savings and bundled knowledge do. Weather, city search and opening web sources require internet access.

What changed about slip records?
Slip entry and legacy slip history have been removed. The existing effective counter start and savings are preserved. Legacy slip records are also removed during backup import.

Is the timeline a health prediction?
No. It summarizes general research findings. Neither the counter nor Health values confirm personal recovery. The app does not replace healthcare.

Immediate danger

If health or life is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services. In the Czech Republic, call 155 or 112. This app does not replace emergency help.

Alcohol withdrawal safety

Suddenly reducing or stopping alcohol can be medically dangerous for some people. If you drink daily or heavily, or have had withdrawal symptoms before, do not proceed without assessment by a clinician. This app is not a tapering or detox guide and does not determine a safe approach.

Quitline in the Czech Republic

Czech National Quitline: 800 350 000 (free, weekdays 10:00–18:00). This is a non-emergency professional consultation service for people who want to change addictive behaviour. In immediate danger, call 155 or 112.
