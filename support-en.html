What the app does

  • privately tracks time from a date you enter,
  • stores short personal journal notes,
  • provides a general educational map with links to public research sources,
  • offers an optional Face ID or device-passcode lock,
  • includes six visual styles.

What the app does not do

  • it is not a medical or emergency service,
  • it does not measure health or provide a diagnosis,
  • it does not provide treatment, detox, or dosing,
  • it does not upload your records to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?
Only on this iPhone, in the app's encrypted storage.

How do I delete it?
In the app: Records → About & Privacy → Delete all data. You can also uninstall the app.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Internet is used only when you choose to open an external source in Safari.

Do I need an account?
No.

Immediate danger

If health or life is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services. In the Czech Republic, call 155 or 112. This app does not replace emergency help.
