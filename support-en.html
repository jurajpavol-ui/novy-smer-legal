What the app does

  • privately tracks time from a date you enter,
  • stores short personal journal notes,
  • provides a general educational map with links to public research sources,
  • offers an optional Face ID or device-passcode lock,
  • includes eight visual styles.

Specific features

  • one or more journeys with your own start date,
  • check-ins and short notes,
  • slips — records of a return,
  • urge events with optional intensity (0–10) and triggers,
  • optional costs and savings,
  • pledges / if-then intentions,
  • an optional SOS contact (name and/or phone),
  • versioned acknowledgements (18+, methodology, and any alcohol warning) with timestamps,
  • manual JSON backup export and import through the iOS share sheet.

How your data is stored

  • encrypted app storage (AES-GCM, with the key in the iOS Keychain),
  • the app file is excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups,
  • no account, cloud synchronization, advertising, or analytics,
  • data leaves the device only through actions you initiate (opening an external link, dialing a number, or saving your own JSON backup),
  • an exported JSON backup is not protected by the app-storage encryption; you are responsible for storing it safely,
  • an imported backup does not carry over the 18+, methodology, or alcohol acknowledgements; current notices must be accepted again.

What the app does not do

  • it is not a medical or emergency service,
  • it does not measure health or provide a diagnosis,
  • it does not provide treatment, detox, or dosing,
  • it does not upload your records to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?
Normally only on this iPhone, in the app's encrypted storage. Data leaves the app only when you export a JSON backup or initiate an external action.

How do I delete it?
In the app: Records → About & Privacy → Delete all data. You can also uninstall the app.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Internet is used only when you choose to open an external source in Safari.

Do I need an account?
No.

Immediate danger

If health or life is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services. In the Czech Republic, call 155 or 112. This app does not replace emergency help.

Alcohol withdrawal safety

Suddenly reducing or stopping alcohol can be medically dangerous for some people. If you drink daily or heavily, or have had withdrawal symptoms before, do not proceed without assessment by a clinician. This app is not a tapering or detox guide and does not determine a safe approach.

Quitline in the Czech Republic

Czech National Quitline: 800 350 000 (free, weekdays 10:00–18:00). This is a non-emergency professional consultation service for people who want to change addictive behaviour. In immediate danger, call 155 or 112.
